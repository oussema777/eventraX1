## Users

Event organizers and attendees across B2B professional contexts. Organizers create and manage conferences, trade missions, pitching events, outreach campaigns, and networking events. Attendees discover, register for, and engage with events. Both audiences are often Africa-focused startups, accelerators, and international professionals operating across EN/FR/AR markets.

When organizers land on the homepage, they're evaluating whether Eventra can handle their next event. They need to feel confident in the platform's capability within seconds. When attendees arrive, they're looking for upcoming events or were sent a link; they need clarity and a fast path to discovery or registration.

## Product Purpose

Eventra is a B2B event management and networking platform that lets organizers create, customize, and launch professional events with built-in design studios, registration forms, speaker management, session scheduling, and attendee networking. It replaces the fragmented workflow of multiple tools (Eventbrite for ticketing, Canva for design, Google Forms for registration) with one integrated system.

Success looks like: an organizer going from zero to a published, branded event landing page in under 15 minutes, with attendees registering and networking seamlessly.

## Brand Personality

**Bold, Professional, Energetic.**

Eventra speaks with confidence, not arrogance. The tone is direct and action-oriented: "Create your event" not "Get started on your journey." Energy comes from purposeful color, strong typography, and decisive layout, not from decoration or animation excess. Professional means polished and trustworthy, not corporate or stiff.

Think: the energy of a well-run conference lobby. People moving with purpose, clear signage, a buzz of activity, everything working smoothly.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS landing page templates.** No cookie-cutter hero + three feature cards + testimonials + CTA footer. If it looks like it came from a Framer or Webflow template marketplace, it's wrong. Every section should feel intentional and specific to Eventra.
- **Flat marketplace listings.** Not Eventbrite's utilitarian card grid. Events are experiences, not inventory.
- **Decoration without purpose.** No floating gradient blobs, abstract 3D shapes, or background patterns that don't communicate anything.

## Design Principles

1. **Confidence in every pixel.** UI should feel like it was built by people who've run events, not people who've read about them. Expert-level defaults, no hand-holding.
2. **Speed is a feature.** Every interaction should feel immediate. Fast load, fast comprehension, fast action. Remove friction before adding features.
3. **Show the product, not the promise.** Prefer real screenshots, actual event examples, and live data over abstract illustrations and vague benefit statements.
4. **International by design.** EN/FR/AR and RTL aren't afterthoughts. Layout, typography, and copy must work across all three without feeling adapted.
5. **Earn attention, don't demand it.** Strong visual hierarchy guides the eye naturally. No aggressive animations, no auto-playing videos, no pop-ups fighting for attention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Ensure minimum 4.5:1 contrast for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI components. Full keyboard navigation support. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Screen reader compatible with proper ARIA landmarks and labels. RTL layout support for Arabic is a first-class requirement, not a bolt-on.
