The Problem
Africa's event industry is booming.
The tools weren't built for it.
Fragmented tools: Organizers stitch together 4–5 platforms — registration, website, emails, networking, ticketing — wasting time and budget.
Western-only platforms: Eventbrite, Hopin, Bizzabo are English-first, priced $500–$18K/yr, ignoring multilingual audiences and B2B trade missions.
No digital infrastructure: Africa-Europe trade missions, investment summits, and innovation forums still rely on spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups.
THE GAP: No affordable, multilingual, all-in-one event platform exists for the Africa-Europe-MENA corridor.
